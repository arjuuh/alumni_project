ASSOCIATION OF NAS COLLEGE KANHANGAD

The association was established as a non-profit organization to assist the alumni, students and faculty of Nehru Arts and Science College Kanhangad. The association, together with the management of the College, is dedicated towards strengthening the links between the College and its alumni all across the world.
